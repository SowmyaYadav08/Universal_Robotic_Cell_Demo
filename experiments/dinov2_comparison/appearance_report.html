Assembly Appearance Comparison

Question: Do the two models notice visual differences in the same assembly area?

MobileNet and DINOv2 examine identical pictures. This page compares how alike those pictures look to each model. It does not measure drop-detection accuracy.

1. Compare the mounting area

How to read the score: closer to 1 means more similar to that model; a lower score means more different. A score of 0.85 does not mean 85% accurate or 85% correctly mounted.

__PERFORMANCE__

What can we say?

Both models produced visual features from the same inputs. Compare each model against its own normal variation. A lower raw score from DINOv2 does not, by itself, make it a better detector.

Next, test more checked normal and failure examples at matching assembly stages before choosing a model or setting an alarm threshold.
